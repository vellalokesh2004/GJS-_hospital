Privacy Policy

1. Commitment to Patient Privacy

GJS Multi Specialty Hospital, situated at MIG-223, Opp. ICICI Bank, Mithilapuri VUDA Colony, Madhurawada, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, respects the confidentiality of personal and clinical information shared by patients, visitors, and website users.

2. Information Collection

Information collected through our website enquiry forms is strictly limited to basic patient contact details (such as name, phone number, optional email address, requested medical department, and appointment preferences). We do not collect sensitive clinical records, financial credentials, or medical history through general online contact forms.

3. Use of Information

Contact information submitted through this website is utilized solely for responding to patient enquiries, scheduling consultations, coordinating inpatient admissions, and providing hospital location guidance.
