The Program

What Is Hypertension Management?

Our hypertension management program is a comprehensive, long‑term partnership with a board‑certified cardiologist. We don’t just prescribe pills — we assess your unique blood pressure patterns, identify contributing factors, and create a multi‑faceted plan that combines evidence‑based medication, nutrition, exercise, stress reduction, and routine monitoring.

Thorough evaluation of secondary causes
Medication optimization & minimisation of side effects
Lifestyle modifications tailored to your routine
Home BP monitoring training & ongoing data review

Our Approach

A Personalized BP Control Plan

We address hypertension from every angle — medication, lifestyle, and continuous monitoring — to achieve lasting results.

Medication Management

We fine‑tune your medications to achieve target BP while minimising side effects, using the latest guidelines and, when possible, simplifying your regimen.

Lifestyle Coaching

Evidence‑based guidance on DASH diet, sodium reduction, exercise, weight management, stress reduction, and sleep hygiene – designed to fit your life.

Continuous Monitoring

We teach you to use a home BP monitor correctly and review your log regularly. Telemedicine check‑ins keep you on track between visits.

Process

Four steps to sustainable blood pressure control.

FAQ

Hypertension Questions

Common questions about managing high blood pressure with CardioCare.

What is considered high blood pressure?
According to the American Heart Association, a consistent reading of 130/80 mmHg or higher is considered hypertension. We'll help you understand your specific target and what it means for your health.
Can lifestyle changes really replace medication?
For many patients, yes — especially with early‑stage hypertension. Weight loss, the DASH diet, reduced sodium, regular exercise, and stress management can significantly lower BP. However, some people will still need medication, and we'll guide you on the right balance.
How often will I need follow‑up visits?
Initially, every 2–4 weeks until your BP stabilises. Once controlled, check‑ins can be spaced to every 3–6 months, with telemedicine options available for medication reviews and BP log analysis.
Do you provide home BP monitors?
We recommend and help you select a validated home BP monitor. We'll train you on proper technique and can review your readings remotely. We also offer ambulatory 24‑hour BP monitoring for a more complete picture.
Is hypertension management covered by insurance?
Yes, most insurance plans cover hypertension consultations, monitoring, and medically necessary tests. Our billing team can verify your coverage before your appointment.
