Hospital Overview

About GJS Multi Specialty Hospital

Established in Madhurawada, Visakhapatnam to provide patient-focused healthcare, modern medical infrastructure, and continuous emergency support.

Care Philosophy

Patient-Focused Healthcare in Madhurawada

GJS Multi Specialty Hospital operates with a foundational commitment to providing ethical, transparent, and patient-centered clinical care. Situated opposite ICICI Bank in Mithilapuri VUDA Colony, Madhurawada, our hospital serves the growing community of Visakhapatnam with essential and emergency healthcare services.

We believe that every patient deserves dedicated medical attention, clear communication, and access to clean, modern clinical facilities. Our multidisciplinary framework enables coordinated care from primary evaluations to intensive inpatient management.

Hospital Capacity & Infrastructure

Comprehensive Inpatient & Surgical Infrastructure

Our hospital facility is planned to support both immediate emergency requirements and planned medical treatments.

50-Bed Inpatient Capacity

Organized into private suite rooms, single rooms, general wards, and sharing wards, offering versatile accommodation suited to varied patient needs.

Critical Care & Surgery

Equipped with a monitored Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and an advanced operation theatre featuring laparoscopic surgical systems.

Diagnostic & Support Units

Clinical laboratory, 12-lead ECG, ultrasound sonography, 24-hour ambulance services, 24/7 pharmacy, and vaccination facilities.
