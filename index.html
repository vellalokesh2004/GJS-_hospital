About Our Hospital

Dedicated Healthcare with Accessible, Reliable Clinical Services

GJS Multi Specialty Hospital is established to bring accessible, dependable medical care to the residents of Mithilapuri VUDA Colony, Madhurawada, and the greater Visakhapatnam region. Located conveniently opposite ICICI Bank, the hospital integrates multi-disciplinary medical specialities under one roof.

With 50 inpatient beds spanning private suites to general wards, round-the-clock emergency support, an Intensive Care Unit, advanced operation theatre with laparoscopy, and round-the-clock experienced nursing, our hospital ensures patient-centric care at every stage.
