GJS Multi Specialty Hospital operates a 24/7 emergency department and a 24-hour ambulance service. In any acute medical emergency or trauma situation, please call our direct hotline at 7981225781. Our emergency bay is equipped for immediate triage, resuscitation, and seamless transfer into our Intensive Care Unit (ICU) or advanced operation theatre if needed.

The hospital is conveniently situated in Madhurawada, opposite ICICI Bank in Mithilapuri VUDA Colony, easily accessible by public and private transport. You can also view Google Maps Directions.

GJS Hospital provides a total capacity of 50 inpatient beds structured across multiple categories to accommodate diverse patient needs:

  • Suite Rooms: Premium private patient suite with dedicated attendant seating and en-suite amenities.
  • Single Rooms: Individual private rooms for focused recovery and quiet patient care.
  • General Wards: Clean, well-ventilated shared ward beds with privacy partitions and nurse oversight.
  • Sharing Wards: Semi-private twin sharing rooms.
  • Intensive Care Unit (ICU): Continuous vital parameter monitoring and critical life support.

You can enquire for an outpatient consultation or planned admission through multiple direct channels:

  • Direct Telephone: Call 7981225781 for instantaneous confirmation.
  • Email: Write to gjshospitalvizag@gmail.com.
  • Online Form: Fill out the client enquiry form on our Contact Us page or Homepage.
